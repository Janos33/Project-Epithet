About Project Epithet

Discover the words that define your writing. Project Epithet is a graph-based NLP engine that attempts to describe you based on your work, and also compare you to others.

The poems are processed entirely within your browser using decentralized client-side statistical processing. Because your text is converted into abstract numerical embeddings locally on your own device, the raw poetry or prose you write is never sent across the internet or stored on external servers, ensuring complete privacy and data anonymity.
